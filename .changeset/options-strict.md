Every function that takes an options object reads it one way and refuses an option it does not take, whatever its type, so that a misspelled option or limit never passes unseen: `parseXml`, `resolveIncludes`, `findAttribute`, `buildElement`, `createTermConverter`, `createReadError`, `locateElement`, the readers of `asi`, `metadata` and `package`, `readPackageFile`, `validateAsi`, `validateTest`, `validateResults`, `validatePackage`, `validatePnpNamespace`, `validateXmlFiles`, `resolveContent`, `selectCard`, `deriveHazardExposure`, `readNumericRecord`, `compileXsdRegex`, `writeWireValue`, the readers and writers of `item-formats`, `createScoringOperators`, `compareMathSteps`, `readResultsJson`, `buildCatMetadata`, the functions of `delivery` (`createEngine`, its `start`, `planTest`, `listPlanItems`, `timeLimits`, `readTestInputs`, `startItemSession`, `resolveStandaloneSessionControl`, `readCandidateAction`) and `createItemVariables`; `createToolScoreIntake` of the player, which refused no unknown option before. `createLocalSession` and `createSessionHost` read `maxIdleMs` and `clock` with the schemas that read their other options. A limit that is misspelled in `xmlLimits`, `limits` of a package, an operator or a reader is refused as well: those four readers of limits were one function in four forms, two of which let it pass. The messages of a refused option name the path of the option (`The options object is invalid at limits.maxBytes: expected a value of at least 1, found 0.`). `@assesskit/runtime/checks` has `objectSchema` and `abortSignalSchema`, and the engine passes only `customOperators` and `resolveTemplate` to the static checks.
