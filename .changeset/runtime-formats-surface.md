Fewer names, the same capabilities. Added: `RUBRIC_KINDS` of `asi`, from which `RubricKind` is derived. Moved out of the public entries to `xml/internal`, `interactions/internal`, `content/internal` and `pnp/internal`, which the runtime's own readers and delivery call: `ElementReader`, `ReaderRules`, `createTermConverter`, `createReadError`, `locateElement` and `isXmlLanguage` (and the options types of the last four), `XMLNS_NAMESPACE` and `XSI_NAMESPACE` of `xml`; `presentChoices` and `deriveAnsweredStatus` of `interactions`; `isShown` and `HAZARD_TERMS` of `content`; `isSetSupportName` and `deriveExtensionName` of `pnp`. Removed: `deriveInitialResponse` and `shuffleChoices`, the checked wrappers of what delivery calls; `listCatalogs`; `writePackageFile` (write what `writePackage` returns); `PciJson` (use `JsonValue`); `DrdHazardTerm` (use `DrdHazard` of `metadata`); `MANIFEST_SCHEMA_VERSION`; the five call types `ExpressionCall`, `NumberCall`, `TextCall`, `HotspotCall` and `ReadableCall`, which `OperatorCall` now spells out. Renamed: `PnpHazardTerm` is `PnpHazard`, of `pnp`, beside `HazardTerm`. `OperatorName` and `OPERATOR_NAMES` now come from the keys of `OPERATOR_DEFINITIONS`.

`compileScoring` takes `portable`, `floor` and `allParts`; its options `limits` and `feedback` are gone. Its keys are held to `DEFAULT_KEY_LIMITS`, whose `responses` rises from 64 to 256, the count at which the worst item ends in 224 MiB, within the budget, and `FEEDBACK` is always written. `WriteItemsOptions.limits` is `{ maxAnswers }` alone, as the writers read nothing else, and refuses any other limit. The downloads of the command line take no `answerMs`, `stallMs` or `tries`, and `downloadText` and `downloadFile` are no longer in the package.
